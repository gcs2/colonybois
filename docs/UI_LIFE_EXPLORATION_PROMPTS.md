# UI and living-world follow-up prompts

## Wildlife asset directions

Use case: stylized-concept. A practical alien wildlife asset direction board matching the restrained stylized real-time 3D fidelity of the reference game image. Input image is a fidelity/material/palette reference, not a scene to reproduce. Three equal panels, each with a large readable creature/plant, a small behavior vignette, and its useful collectible material. Warm clay/sage ground, matte sculpted forms, mint accents, subtle contact shadows, simple geometry, clean focus. Original creepy-cute anatomy, not earth animals in costumes, not smiling cartoon faces or elaborate high-detail monsters. All forms should be feasible as low-poly meshes and a few animation bones. No HUD, no inventory windows.
Left panel 'BELL GRAZER': improve the tiny peach umbrella walkers from the reference into an appealing strange animal. A translucent-looking but opaque-renderable peach ribbed bell mantle protects a small plum-colored body, four short articulated legs and a pair of expressive dark bead eyes tucked beneath the rim. Clearly visible fleshy body, not just an umbrella on sticks. Two small side vignettes: lowering its soft feeding tube toward a lantern plant; startled mantle folding closed over its eyes. A pale shed bell membrane lies on a nearby rock as its nonlethal collectible. Title below 'Shed membrane'.
Middle panel 'BRINE SIPHON': a squat sage amphibious alien beside the dark teal pool, broad soft body with three stubby suction feet, two short eye stalks, a folded throat sac and a small curled siphon mouth. Slightly uncanny but endearing, expressive through posture. Behavior vignette drinking, throat sac gently expanded; small mineral-encrusted pearl deposited beside the pool. No gore or dissection. Title below 'Filter pearls'.
Right panel 'LANTERN REED': a cluster of five mint pear-shaped plant pods on jointed maroon stalks, a distinctive root bulb and broad curled protective leaves. Show one heavy ripe pod and one cutaway diagram of an intact harvested pod revealing a simple layered luminous gel interior, not realistic wet gore. Small vignette of a bell grazer feeding from the stalk, plant closes two leaves in response. Title below 'Lantern gel'.
Keep anatomy simple and visually distinct at gameplay distance. No fur, scales, complex tentacle simulation, fantasy armor, huge horns, dozens of species, or implied open-world content expansion. This board defines three candidate assets and behaviors, not three fully implemented systems.

Reference: `artifacts/visual-targets-20260923/surface-v1.png`.

Built-in imagegen. Candidate UI directions; no selection has been approved. The preceding flat Jump card was rejected. Surface environment fidelity was endorsed; creatures and interface were not.

## Interface alternatives

Use case: ui-mockup. A side-by-side comparison board with THREE radically different original spaceship-game navigation UI aesthetics, each shown as a large close-up crop at usable interface scale. Wide landscape. This is a two-day-buildable Godot UI design exercise, using simple layered shapes and a few custom textures, not cinematic dashboard concept art.
All three panels show exactly the SAME interaction: select a warm star, a ship-centred jump-range arc, selected star distance '2.4 pc', engine reach '3 pc', a tactile icon affordance positioned beside the star, compact hull/energy gauges. NO rectangular Jump button or destination card. No minimap, elaborate cockpit, copper, bevelled chrome, generic web-app panels or pill buttons. A star stays visually a star, not a giant planet. The background is near-black space with a few pinpoints, not a lavish painted nebula.
Panel A label '01 / FIELD INSTRUMENTS': tangible matte bone-white angular ceramic instrument pieces with deep charcoal inset gauges, mustard and vermilion details, tiny stamped numerals and notched edges. Mid-century scientific exploration equipment reinterpreted for curious alien explorers. Strong physical identity, no rounded boxes, no ornamental framing. A small triangular ochre launch tab sits adjacent to the selected star.
Panel B label '02 / SIGNAL CUTOUTS': bold flat screen-printed graphics, expressive cut-paper geometry, coral / ultramarine / acid yellow, deep ink background, large unmistakable directional symbols, segmented block gauges, energetic asymmetry with precise alignment. Minimal texture, absolutely no glow, gradients or bevels. Inventive adult playful alien science aesthetic rather than a children's toy. Travel affordance is a small sharp double-chevron next to the selected star.
Panel C label '03 / OPTICAL NAVIGATION': very sparse optical-instrument markings directly in space, fine ivory radial ticks, small cyan/magenta interference accents, highly legible numerals, no background plates. Selected star has an incomplete angular reticle and a short elegant directional pointer. Gauges are tiny separated segmented strokes along the screen edge. Travel affordance integrated into the reticle, no large visible text control. Technical but graceful, avoid a generic military HUD.
Use crisp modern typography with distinct font weights and thoughtful alignment. Text only panel labels and the two numeric range labels specified. Each panel must look substantially different in construction, not like three palette swaps. Sharp focus, orthographic screenshot style.
