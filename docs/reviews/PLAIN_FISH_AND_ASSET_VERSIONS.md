## Exact prompt

Create a restrained character concept image: one entirely ordinary-looking small goldfish suspended in clear water inside a perfectly plain round transparent floating orb. The fish is convincingly fishlike: simple orange-gold scales, small normal lateral eyes, ordinary fins and tail, a tiny natural fish mouth, neutral unaware-looking expression. NOT a cartoon character, no human face, no huge baby eyes, no unusual fins, no alien sensory appendages. The orb is just a simple clear sphere, with a subtle curved edge and delicate reflection that leaves the fish plainly visible; no outer mechanism, no stand, no ring, no seams or handles. It hovers a little above a quiet matte warm-gray floor, with a soft subtle shadow underneath. Whole sphere visible with generous empty space, centered three-quarter fish profile. Clean softly lit stylized 3D game concept render, modest detail, credible water and glass, no dramatic lighting. Absolutely NO tools, grippers, thrusters, hardware, armor, robes, weapons, jewelry, plants, coral, stones, decorations, other creatures, text, labels, borders, bubbles clutter, energy effects, glowing eyes, ominous aura or power indicators. The idea is pure understatement: this tiny harmless-looking ordinary fish could secretly be extremely powerful, but NOTHING in the picture reveals that. The contrast should be left entirely to imagination. Do not embellish or try to make it look powerful or villainous. One plain fish, one plain floating orb, nothing else.

## Versions versus quality

User asks to retain versions simultaneously and consider different quality levels. Preserve alternatives non-destructively with distinct filenames and a current-selection record. Existing images and V1/V2 model sources already coexist. Rejected/candidate renders remain local; approved source references and reproducible model source are backed up. Do not call a stored hash an image backup.

Three independent axes: design option (different look), maturity (concept/blockout/finished/rigged), and runtime quality (mesh/texture/shader/animation-cost variants of one accepted asset). A rough blockout is not the finished character's Low graphics setting. Imagegen returns raster artwork; generating a 'low quality' picture does not derive a coherent 3D asset. Build an accepted master model and derive reviewed optimized variants from it. Godot can generate mesh LODs for imported 3D scenes; skinned models may need corrections. [Godot mesh LOD documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/3d/mesh_lod.html). Shared identity/silhouette/rig should remain consistent across quality presets. Do not load every design revision into the shipped game.
