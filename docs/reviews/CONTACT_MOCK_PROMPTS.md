## Independent critic and remaining corrections

The critic compared v1 with actual contact/shop, Spore v2 contact/shop and implementation. It is a worthwhile composition candidate, not final UI acceptance. The representative inhabits a room; the greeting and primary choices fit without scrolling. v2 resolves the three targeted icon/border problems; it does not resolve these remaining issues:

- Dock services must depend on actual dock range/access. Remote contact offers Approach dock or an unavailable reason, never instantaneous service.
- Relationship pips need a neutral reference and actual value/reason tooltip, not an invented six-tier relationship model.
- Identity details must retain Oolun / Exchange delegate, with government and philosophy separate.
- Symmetrical round eyes and smile feel overly plush. More intentional gaze/asymmetry and authored gestures should preserve creepy-cute individuality. The authored flask is amber; this candidate makes it green. Restore or explicitly settle the asset specification before production.
- The bright planet competes with the conversation; modest contrast reduction is preferable to masking hazards with blur.
- Hull/energy fills must come from real ratios and share presentation with flight. Static pictured bars are not a data implementation.
- Trade continuity, first/repeat encounter, locked/embargo/refusal, existing danger, hover/focus and reduced motion still require companion states. Static text and pictures cannot prove the actor performs, the controls work or the audio feels good.
