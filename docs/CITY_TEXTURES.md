# Generated city material candidates

Tool: built-in `image_gen`. Original PNGs preserved; copies integrated as base-color textures via cached Godot materials, triplanar mapping and mipmapped filtering. These are color textures, not complete physically based material sets. No normal/roughness maps have been generated. Tiling was requested, but production seam cleanup and human art approval remain open.

Files: `assets/materials/shell-wall-v1.png`, `scale-roof-v1.png`, `civic-paving-v1.png`. Used on the courtyard kit and background city. Advisor portraits and prompts: ADVISOR_ART.md.

## wall

Generate a single seamless tileable square PBR-style base-color texture for a stylized creepy-cute alien city game, pale warm ivory and muted sage ceramic architectural panels grown from shell-like material. Subtle large irregular rounded rectangular modules, tiny seams, restrained handpainted surface mottling, clean urban workmanship, charming organic material but disciplined construction. Flat orthographic texture, uniformly lit, no perspective, no shadows baked in, no directional lighting, no border, no lettering, no windows, no buildings, no environment. Low contrast for miniature city readability. The whole image is the repeating material; not a swatch board.

## roof

Generate a single seamless tileable square base-color texture for a stylized alien city game's roofs: desaturated deep teal overlapping rounded ceramic scale shingles, gently scalloped interlocking rows with fine pale oxidized edges. Disciplined architectural repeating pattern, subtle tactile handpainted charm, clean rather than ruined, cute and peculiar science-fiction craft. Flat orthographic texture, evenly lit, no strong shadows or perspective, no text, no border, no objects, not a material ball or swatch board. Medium low contrast readable in a miniature city. Entire image is the material.

## paving

Generate one seamless tileable square base-color texture of warm grey-sage alien civic paving: orderly large rounded hexagonal stone slabs with slender dark grout, occasional tiny muted turquoise mineral flecks. Stylized handpainted restrained detail, clean modern alien urban craftsmanship. Flat orthographic evenly lit texture with no objects, vegetation, shadows, perspective, borders, labels, lettering or street markings. Not a swatch board. Low contrast; whole image is one repeating ground material for a miniature city.
