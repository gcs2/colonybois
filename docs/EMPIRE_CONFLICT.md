# Empire hostilities and colony defense

23 September 2026. W01 integrated raid/defense pilot; conquest, territorial surrender and empire destruction remain open. Coloring and sculpting remain skipped.

## Playing this checkpoint

The shield/arrow icon beneath the rank readout opens **Colony defense**. It is also available through Colony administration and Communications → Conflict. Hover controls for costs and restrictions. A declaration takes a review click followed by a separate confirmation naming the consequences. Inspection pauses the shared simulation and cancels attack orders.

- **Escalation:** a contacted empire at −50 relations or worse, without a non-aggression treaty or alliance, gives a 90-second warning. Improve relations above −50 to avert war. War ends that empire's trade, non-aggression and alliance agreements, closes its markets/transit and recalls its escort. Gifts and ordinary reconciliation cannot substitute for a wartime peace settlement.
- **Inbound raids:** war can produce one active raid across this sector at a time. The targeted colony receives 90 seconds' notice. An empire waits at least 600 seconds between dispatches. The Directorate prefers vulnerable ports; the Consortium weights stored goods; the Commune weights strategic climate alteration. Ties follow stable colony ordering. These are bounded authored priorities, not a strategic economy generating invasion fleets.
- **Personal response:** plot a route from the warning panel, pay the normal journey costs, enter the threatened orbit and click its attacking ship. Approach stops within weapon range and uses the existing arc lance's damage, upgrades, ten-energy cost and shared cooldown. Steering, Stop or inspection cancels the attack. Raid fire marks a 4 m aim volume for two seconds before applying 14 hull damage. Allied escorts assist the selected target and suffer real persistent hull loss and diplomatic consequences.
- **Prepared defense:** commission a battery for 180 Marks and 60 local materials. Its twelve rounds each deal 12 damage, one shot every six seconds. It operates while the flagship is away. Ammunition is finite; rearming costs 60 Marks and 20 materials. The Directorate's 120-hull raid takes ten battery hits; its intervening bombardments still damage the port. Defense cannot be commissioned, repaired or rearmed during active bombardment.
- **Consequences:** every fifteen seconds an attacker removes 20 of the port's 100 integrity. At zero, the raid leaves a disabled port. Ship services, outpost production, freight through that source/destination and strategic colony exports stop. Goods already in a carrier are retained. Repair costs 80 Marks and 20 local materials. Home recharge remains free once the port is operational. A persistent HUD warning identifies disabled ports. This raid damages port infrastructure, not population, buildings or ownership.
- **Peace:** 250 Marks, reduced by 50 per successful defense against that empire to a floor of 100, withdraws its raid and establishes a 300-second truce. Former treaties remain ended, relations become −10, and port damage remains. Peace gives no combat badge credit. Each distinct successfully defended colony contributes once to Defender; repeated raids cannot farm that accomplishment.

An orbital port mesh follows [the asset specification](../art/specs/colony_defense_port.md) and uses the real service approach. Its light/armor states reflect damage; paired emitters appear only after a battery is commissioned. Raid ships reuse the existing original modular hostile kit in faction colors. The enemy hull label, aim volume, personal/allied beams and port bombardment/battery flashes read actual combat records. Existing sound cues are reused; final music, combat sound and voice production are still open.

## Authority and persistence

`empire_conflict.gd` owns wars, warning/truce clocks, the active raid, each port's integrity and ammunition, distinct defended worlds and a monotonically increasing raid ID. `expedition_session.gd` advances it on the same clock as travel, economy and encounters. Enemy local fire uses the flagship and fleet's existing hull, weapons and loss paths; offscreen bombardment and batteries remain aggregate. No per-person simulation or independent tactical battle scene was introduced.

Snapshot v16 validates and restores conflict alongside the field, economy, colonies, freight, agreements, escorts and chronicle before replacing live state. Older campaigns gain no fabricated wars or defense rewards. War/raid events record faction identity, target planet, deadlines, damage and outcomes with causal links. Simulated time stops during pause/inspection, not on view changes or ordinary travel. The sector's blocked-port list is derived from conflict integrity before its economy tick and is not a second damage record.

## Evidence and remaining work

The complete regression suite passed with the initial 65 conflict checks. The expanded **79-check** conflict suite also passed, including a real paid kit/deployment/construction/freight fixture, offworld port loss, cargo conservation, actual ally recruitment/fire/loss, saved mid-raid state, atomic invalid-save rejection, deterministic continuation, paid repair/peace and real mouse/button wiring. Actual warning, response, combat, weapon and damage screens were captured at 1080p and 1440p using `tools/CaptureConflict.gd`. These verify implementation and layout, not native input feel, balance or AAA presentation acceptance.

This is still missing conquest/surrender, building destruction, war objectives, rival resource-funded fleet production, enemy-vs-enemy wars, allied attack requests, empire extinction and the wider reference weapon/defense catalog. The single-raid cap and fixed warning are scenario staging; no graph-based enemy fleet journey is claimed. The battery is a bounded aggregate installation, not Spore's full turret/Uber Turret behavior. The three faction ships vary hull, color and target priorities; they are not a finished fleet-role roster. Native response-time, economy and combat playtests remain necessary.

This pilot adapts those links; its timings, prices and port-outage rules are original scenario tuning, not verified retail Spore values.
