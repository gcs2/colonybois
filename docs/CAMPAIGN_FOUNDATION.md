# Campaign foundation

The current foundation is consolidated in two documents:

- [Current story treatment](STORY_CURRENT.md): the forgotten colony, the Vanguard, national politics, interstellar discoveries and the fall of the United Nations of Earth.
- [Project vision](PROJECT_VISION.md): the complete gameplay direction, branching abilities, simulation limits, sandbox, art and implementation boundaries.

These supersede this document's first city and currency proposal. The opening now targets a modern city of approximately **120,000**, with a readable street grid and SimCity 4-like urban fidelity. Population and services are aggregates, not individual citizen simulations. Money is **Marks**. Latch and South Loop remain provisional names.

The original outpost is the society's ancient origin, not the size or technological level of the present city. Spaceflight already exists. Taking national leadership does not unify the planet. The exact canonical ending and the player species' ancestry remain undecided.

The small playable colony prototype is separate from the proposed campaign. See [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) for the next bounded implementation gate and [City concept](CITY_CONCEPT.md) for the latest visual study.
