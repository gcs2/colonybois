## Spore reference, separated from our interpretation

Spore has ten player archetypes and eight philosophies. The usual NPC empire set comprises Bard, Diplomat, Ecologist, Scientist, Shaman, Trader, Warrior and Zealot; Knight and Wanderer are not ordinary randomly encountered NPC archetypes. Archetype affects powers and relationships as well as presentation. [SporeWiki: Philosophy](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Philosophy). Indexed article text was available; direct retrieval of this overview returned an error. No new motion/audio verification follows from reading the wiki.

Harmony specifically belongs to Shaman; Prosperity to Trader; Order to Diplomat. Shaman NPCs tend toward peaceful behavior. Trader empires are welcoming and profit-oriented. Therefore a merchant who values harmony is a coherent individual; his profession does not require changing his entire empire's philosophy. [Shaman](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Shaman), [Trader](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Trader_(philosophy)), [Diplomat](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Diplomat_(philosophy)). Shaman and Diplomat claims use indexed excerpts; Trader full page was inspected.

The Trader article exposes voice references under neutral, angry, happy, laugh, gasp, urgent, sad and afraid. That gives us an audition inventory, not listening evidence. We have not heard or evaluated those performances. Do not reuse the game's recordings or equate captions with the performance.

## Our character architecture

Subsequent explicit request: map characters to the philosophies and create one cast sheet. PHILOSOPHY_CAST_PROMPT.md records the resulting proposal: LEFT-derived merchant for Prosperity, RIGHT-derived harbor keeper for Harmony, plus six distinct individuals. This is a later deliberate mapping, not a reinterpretation of the user's earlier visual comment. No saved faction attributes have changed.

Keep species anatomy, national government, public philosophy, individual occupation, personal temperament and current relationship separate. Those fields jointly inform behavior; none determines the whole personality. Two nations of one species can disagree; two representatives of one philosophy can be warm and cold respectively. Body size does not determine honesty, greed or morality.

For each representative define a desire, a fear, a contradiction, a personal bond, a boundary and a particular way of addressing the player. Then specify gesture, prop, setting and voice. Only after those decisions produce silhouette and expression studies. Avoid creating faces first and attaching a personality afterward.

The reference names below orient the design. All character interpretations and gameplay proposals are ours, not assertions about Spore dialogue or mechanics.

## Tavi: the first character to develop

**Endorsed seed:** a fat, commanding alien merchant, a little arrogant, who sells wares and talks about his family and journeys through the open expanse. User suggests a harmonious temperament. Name Tavi Rill remains a working continuity choice; no new name needed to improve him.

**Proposed foundation:** he built a family trading house by personally flying routes other merchants avoided. He enjoys comfort now, but still knows his ships and cargo. His confidence comes from experience and from taking responsibility for a large extended family. He wants the business to outlive him and has difficulty trusting his children with its dangerous voyages. His adult daughter captains her own freighter and resents his interference. These details are proposals, not established campaign history.

He is proud, expansive and hospitable. He assumes he knows more than you at first, and often does about his own routes. He pays attention when you show competence. A rare material makes him lean forward and examine it; he does not simply praise everything the player brings. He can respect a refusal. He will not sacrifice a crew to maintain a delivery promise.

**Philosophy recommendation:** merchant by occupation, committed to keeping ports and families connected. He can be a Harmony-aligned individual operating inside the existing mercantile Consortium. Do not silently change the Consortium's saved government or philosophy. If a future Harmony nation becomes his home, update affiliation and political authority explicitly. A merchant does not automatically have authority to declare war or bind an empire; the current contact screen's diplomacy/fleet powers need a credible charter or a separate authorized representative.

**Speech direction:** conversational, confident, concrete. No invented accent as a substitute for character, no obscure maxims, no forced punchlines. Adult actor performance with measured pace, occasional emphatic stress, and genuine attention to the other speaker. Warmth need not disappear when refusing a discount. Voice casting and nonverbal alien vocalization remain alternatives to audition; neither has been selected or heard.

Example lines, optional/contextual and not a new mission script:

- First contact: “Tavi Rill. Come in, Captain. Let's see what you've brought.”
- Price challenged: “I've flown that route. Fuel, escorts, port fees—that's what you're paying for.”
- Family: “My daughter brought this shipment in. First voyage under her own command.”
- An old journey: “We lost the main drive out there. Nine days before another ship picked up our signal.”
- Practical warning: “Beyond that marker, you're on your own. Take another energy pack.”
- Loyalty under pressure: “Those freighters have my people aboard. I won't send them through a war zone.”

These lines need context and performance review. Stories are short optional exchanges during a transaction or on request; the player never has to listen to a biography to buy supplies. Routine repeat contact must not replay the family introduction.

**Appearance follows this personality:** a broad, heavy-bodied alien comfortably occupying his trading cabin, strong weight and deliberate movement, genuinely nonhuman eyes and mouth, several useful grasping limbs. One limb arranges samples while another gestures to the visitor. Travel-worn quality clothing, a repaired navigation instrument, family keepsakes and wares say more than extravagant jewelry. His size should convey presence and comfort, not be the joke. No human eyelids/lips painted purple, toy face, copied franchise merchant, or automatic association of bodily size with corruption.

**Acting beats:** first surveys the ship/cargo display, then gives the visitor full attention; occupies space without fidgeting; lifts a sample toward the light; opens his posture when mentioning family; stops handling goods when a crew is threatened. Story beats and emotional reversals should emerge from actual events.

## Make the personality consequential without simulation bloat

Persist a small set of encounter facts: met before, goods traded, agreements kept/broken, convoy incident and current affiliation/authority. Select authored lines and gestures from real command/event results. Do not promise information or service the simulation cannot provide. Proposed convoy introductions need finite stock, route feasibility, costs and risk before shipping.

NPC decisions can use existing faction policy and economic checks with authored priorities. No live language-model dependency or per-citizen simulation is required. One carefully authored character with distinct reactions proves this pipeline before expanding the cast. A read-through and pose sheet precede another polished window mock. User acceptance, native animation and audio listening remain separate gates.
