# Urban tutorial city: revised scale study

![Urban city concept, not gameplay](../artifacts/latch-urban-city-v2.png)

**Unapproved concept study, not a gameplay screenshot.** The image is kept locally under ignored artifacts; its prompt and design notes are backed up here. The previous village-scale study was rejected, archived locally, and removed from active tracked assets. Its earlier prompt remains in Git history.

The new study depicts a modern city of roughly 120,000: continuous streets, apartment blocks, a business center, civic facilities, industry, rail and an outer spaceport. Latch and South Loop are provisional names. Marks is the selected currency name; the illustrated treasury is not a balance target or live game data.

This is a scale and layout reference. It is more conventional and detailed than the intended final creepy-cute world; do not adopt its realism as the character or production-art direction. A generated image does not establish a valid street graph, exact housing capacity or runtime performance. Validate those in the Godot graybox.

The target is SimCity 4-like urban richness using modular geometry, aggregate population and services, and capped decorative traffic. A city of 120,000 must not require 120,000 simulated agents. The first editable district can remain bounded within a larger aggregate city.

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one NEW high-resolution wide 16:9 concept image for an original 3D city-and-space strategy game. This is a decisive art-direction correction: a modern URBAN CITY with a credible population of 120,000, orderly zoning and streets, seen in a classic city-simulation camera. NOT a little outpost, fantasy village, whimsical market, medieval town, chaotic diorama, or city built into giant ruins.
CAMERA AND COMPOSITION: elevated fixed dimetric/orthographic game camera, roughly 45-degree down view, zoomed far enough out to read the coherent plan of an entire medium city. NO foreground characters, no shallow depth of field, no dramatic low camera. Deep sharp focus across the whole image. Urban fabric fills about 85% of the composition, extends naturally beyond two frame edges, and contains many hundreds of buildings over approximately 12 by 12 rectangular city blocks. Maintain clean spatial separation and recognizable straight aligned roads; do not merge streets into buildings. The grid must be obvious at first glance, with continuous north-south and east-west streets, regular intersections, sidewalks, believable block sizes, and a hierarchy of two broad avenues and smaller local streets.
BUILDINGS AND ZONING: modern apartment buildings with repeated window rows, predominantly 6-12 stories around landscaped courtyards; dense mixed-use commercial streets; a compact business district with a handful of 20-30-story office towers, not a megacity of skyscrapers; schools, hospital, sports field, parks aligned within rectangular blocks; a clearly separated industrial/freight district with factories, warehouses and a rail yard. No detached cottage village. Roads should have tiny cars/buses for scale, with a visible elevated passenger rail line along an avenue and stations integrated at sensible intervals.
SPACEFARING WORLD: restrained near-future infrastructure is ordinary here. Place a compact orbital launch/landing complex at the industrial city's distant outer edge, with two small recognizable sleek spacecraft, connected by a proper arterial road and freight rail. The spaceport must not dominate the city. Clean municipal utilities, communications masts and a few advanced transit elements. It is an established technological society descended from an ancient settlement, not scavengers living inside ruins. One small preserved original lander foundation in a civic park may suggest history. A neighboring city is faintly visible in the distance across the plain, suggesting independent countries still exist.
VISUAL STYLE: polished, approachable stylized 3D city-builder rendering with coherent modular architecture, soft bevels and crisp readable forms. Buildings are functionally believable modern structures, not blobs or mushrooms. Warm cream/light-gray walls, restrained teal glazing, terracotta accents and green street trees. Crisp sunny daylight, gentle shadows, subtle alien lavender vegetation only in a few parks and a small pale moon in the sky. Simplicity at district scale, consistent proportions, no excessive ornament or ruins. The game can have creepy-cute alien characters elsewhere, but this image is about city planning and modern urban life. Do not imitate any existing game's specific buildings, logos, interface or characters.
UI: minimal thin navy-and-cream top bar, exact text 'LATCH' then 'Population 120,000' then 'Marks 8,600,000'. Small subtitle 'South Loop · urban tutorial study'. Bottom-left tiny caption 'CONCEPT ART · NOT GAMEPLAY'. No floating labels over every building, no quest card covering the city, no clutter.
The priority is an orderly, legible modern city grid with plausible residential capacity for 120,000 people.
