# Expedition specimens: pilot kit specification

Original creepy-cute botanical and animal silhouettes for ship-scale collection. No realistic cast, stock franchise designs or per-animal simulation. Eighteen species provide the distinct slots needed to exercise three ecosystem tiers across the three landable worlds; this is a functional coverage set, not the enrichment corpus.

Six visual roles: low clustered small flora (1 m), folding ribbon shrubs (2 m), hollow-crowned trees (4 m), manta grazers with lateral fins, squat striders with paired feet, and predators with a hood surrounding a recessed dark mouth. Species share role geometry but differ in proportion, appendage orientation and palette; shape variations must remain readable at collection distance. Eyes use a dark inset and small pale highlight. Avoid angular armor panels and bright metallic materials. Keep saturation restrained against each planet's terrain.

The authored code kit constructs no more than 18 visible representatives, 14 meshes each, with low-segment ellipsoids and a shared material palette per representative. Plants sway from a root pivot. Grazers bob and articulate lateral appendages; striders alternate feet. Predators have a slower hood pulse and head turn. Motion freezes during inspection and stays within a local visual envelope. Representative poses do not determine ecological state or require pathfinding. A single tool beam and selection marker serve scanning, collection and release.

Native clusters occupy deterministic, separated points inside the flight patch. A released specimen appears at the chosen point when establishment completes; permanent placements survive save/load. Plants root at terrain height. Existing populations, finite local collection stock and extinction visibility come from authoritative state. Model-derived 192px portraits are reproduced by tools/BakeSpecimenPortraits.gd. Preview at near/normal/far camera distances and 1080p/1440p. This is a provisional original geometry kit; final art approval and character rig quality remain open.
