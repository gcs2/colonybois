## Deliverables

Create four coordinated 1920×1080 landscape frames plus one contact sheet, all for the same expedition:

1. **System departure:** the same scout selects the Morrow destination; keep the system map distinct from surface navigation.
2. **Orbit:** Morrow is the destination, with the same scout, route, and landing-region cue.
3. **Approach:** an intermediate scale state that brings the same relay and landing region into view.
4. **Surface arrival:** ordinary player-follow gameplay at that same region and relay; show the local surface chart only here.

Across frames, preserve the practical player-follow camera, small scout (about 6% of gameplay-frame width), world-dominant composition, Field Instruments materials, recognizable controls, full pictorial two-row inventory with quantities, top-left notices, top-right Marks, and compact hull/energy. Keep the same campaign and expedition identity. Use the short labels MORROW SYSTEM · Fly, ORBIT · Relay discovered, APPROACH · Survey relay, and SURFACE · Morrow Basin only as proposed mock labels.

Do not invent exact distances, prices, travel times, input prompts, dialogue, or transition timings. Do not add cinematic framing, blur, an oversized scout, a planet chart in orbit, a galaxy/system map on the surface, blue Windows-style panels, rounded icon wells, shiny copper-like borders, or persistent tool captions. The storyboard proposes layout and continuity; it does not specify production animation or audio.
