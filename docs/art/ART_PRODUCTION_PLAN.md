# Art production contract

> Asset specification; not evidence of finished or accepted art. The task board decides whether production is active. [Documentation map](../README.md).

Current scope: original creepy-cute space exploration and compact Field Instruments UI. Cities support that experience. The [old municipal asset plan](../history/CITY_ART_PRODUCTION_2026-09-24.md) remains a deferred case study, not the next production order. The [task board](../TASK_BOARD.md) decides which asset, if any, is next.

## One asset through the full loop

1. **Brief:** gameplay role, actual screen size/camera range, silhouette, anatomy or construction, material references, motion, interaction sockets and runtime budget hypothesis. Separate approved decisions from unknowns.
2. **Concept:** generate or draw candidates; approve one direction. Reconcile front/side/back as needed. A compelling single view is not a reliable turnaround or 3D production asset.
3. **Shape:** author editable geometry; render neutral views in the pinned engine. Judge major form and appeal before accessories and textures. Stop a failing method rather than multiplying detail.
4. **Finish:** purposeful materials, topology/UVs, textures and rig as needed. Technical import success and art acceptance are separate checks.
5. **Performance:** purposeful animations/reactions in the actual player interaction; no claim of lifelike acting from a static render or idle bob.
6. **Integration:** real-scale views and action states in the game; validate sockets, collision, save references and resource costs where relevant.
7. **Acceptance and reuse:** human art/playability review plus measured frame time/memory. Only then derive LODs, variants and a larger kit.

Character modeling is stopped after the [failed fidelity demonstration](../reviews/MODELING_POSTMORTEM_2026-09-24.md). This contract does not authorize a retry. Existing [merchant brief](TAVI_MODEL_HANDOFF.md) and sources are deferred research material, not an accepted production template.

## Provenance and versions

Keep source, selected reference, exact prompt when applicable, revision identity, dimensions/axes, material/mesh counts, asset dependencies, rig/clip list, measured budgets and review decision. Store rejected render binaries locally; keep reproducible sources and approved references in Git. A hash identifies a file but is not a backup of it.

Design alternatives, maturity stages and runtime quality levels are different. A blockout is not a finished asset's Low graphics preset. Derive optimized runtime variants from one accepted master; do not ship every experiment. Reuse compatible materials, rigs and authoring tools without inventing a universal character creator.

## Production boundaries

Godot 4.7.2 and the current renderer remain the target. Generated imagery can supply concepts or authored texture inputs, not automatically complete geometry, rigs or animation. No engine migration, paid subscription, asset purchase or external upload of project material is implied. Approve a concrete price/license before purchases. Budgets and delivery estimates follow actual measured assets, not promises based on image-generation quality.

Read [art direction](ART_DIRECTION.md), [audio production](AUDIO_PRODUCTION.md), [visual feasibility](../research/VISUAL_FIDELITY_FEASIBILITY.md) and the applicable asset brief; link evidence back to its owning review rather than repeating status in this contract.
